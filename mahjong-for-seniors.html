Mahjong365 guide

Mahjong for seniors: large tiles and calm screens

Plenty of mahjong games put tiny tiles on a busy screen and hurry you along. Mahjong365 does the opposite: big tiles with bold symbols, no timer that makes you fail, and nothing that pops up between your levels.

Free to play. Plays offline, no account.

Large tiles you can tell apart

Mahjong365 is mahjong solitaire made for people who want a puzzle that is easy on the eyes. The tiles are large, with bold symbols, and every pair carries its own unique picture, so two tiles that look alike at a glance are easy to tell apart.

If a board still feels small, pinch to zoom on any level. If you like the look of traditional tiles, you can switch on the classic mahjong tile faces. They are optional, so you choose what is comfortable for your eyes.

Made to be held comfortably

The toolbar can be switched to the left-hand side, which helps left-handed players and anyone who holds the phone that way. The game runs on phones, tablets and foldables, and on big screens it shows wide landscape boards. The interface is available in 50 languages.

The Pause button hides the board, so there is no peeking at the pairs while the game waits for you.

No timer, no pop-up ads

There is no timer that makes you fail, so you can play at your own pace. After each level you see how quickly you found your pairs, but speed never decides whether you finish.

The free version shows one small banner at the bottom of the screen and never a pop-up between levels. Extra hints are available through an optional rewarded video, always your choice. One optional lifetime Premium purchase removes all ads and gives unlimited hints. There is no subscription.

Boards that can always be solved

Every board is built to be solvable, so there are no dead ends and no sudden feeling that you wasted ten minutes. Undo is unlimited and free, and Shuffle is free too. If you make a wrong move, take it back. You need no account and no sign-up, and the game plays without WiFi or mobile data. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and only adds cloud save, so you can carry your progress to a new device, and leaderboards.

A fair word about what it is

Mahjong365 is a pleasant puzzle. We do not claim that it improves memory or health, and we do not make medical promises. It is a quiet game with a new level for every day of the year, and if you are new to the rules, the guide to how to play mahjong solitaire takes two minutes. If you want a fast, competitive game with lives and countdowns, this is not that game.
