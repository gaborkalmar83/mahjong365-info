Mahjong365

Privacy policy. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Mahjong365 is an Android mahjong solitaire game made by Kalmarium Services BV, the Netherlands ("we").

What the app stores

Your progress, stars, coins, daily streak, collected stickers, trophies, settings and the game you are currently playing are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-up, and none of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen from Google AdMob, and you can choose to watch a rewarded video ad for extra hints or double coins. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device information and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings, Privacy options. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The one-time Premium purchase removes all ads, and with them all ad-related data collection in the app.

Purchases

The Premium purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We receive no payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can be restored on any device.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the app stores a copy of your progress as a saved game in your Google account so it can follow you to another device, and it can submit your scores to Play Games leaderboards, where your Play Games profile name and scores are visible to other players. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; we receive no copy of it. Signing in is optional and the game works fully without it.

Ratings

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google handles the review; we do not receive any personal data from it.

Problem log

From version 1.1.1 the app keeps a short problem log on your device: error messages, slow frames and moments when the screen froze, each with a time stamp and what the game was doing, up to the last 60 lines. It stays on your device, survives restarts and is never uploaded. Under Settings, Diagnostics you can read the recent lines, clear the log or switch logging off. Only if you tap "Send problem log to developer" does your own mail app open with a draft addressed to kalmariumservices@gmail.com. The draft contains the log plus the app version, Android version, language, number of processor cores, screen size and whether the ad-free upgrade is active. You press Send yourself and can edit or discard it first; if no mail app is found, the report is copied to the clipboard instead. We use it only to find and fix bugs. There is no crash-reporting service or diagnostics SDK in the app.

Notifications

The app does not send push notifications and has no server.

Artwork and AI

The scenery, tile motifs and icons in the game were created with the help of an AI image generator and then edited by us. The fun facts in the game were written with AI assistance and may contain mistakes; please tell us if you spot one. The app itself does not run any AI and sends no data to any AI service.

Website visits

This website counts page views with a cookie-free script that stores nothing on your device and sends only the page address, the referring website and the country (derived from your network address, which is not kept). It is switched off if your browser sends Do Not Track.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. For step-by-step deletion instructions see delete-data.html.
