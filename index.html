Who it is for

Mahjong 365 is made for people who want a puzzle that is easy on the eyes and easy on the nerves.

  • Seniors and anyone tired of tiny tiles and busy screens. Mahjong for seniors.
  • Relaxed players who want a short daily puzzle with their tea or coffee.
  • Anyone who dislikes pop-up ads, timers and pressure.
  • People who play on a phone, a tablet or a foldable, including wide landscape boards.

If you want a fast, competitive game with lives and countdowns, this is not that game.

How to play mahjong solitaire

  1. Tap two identical tiles that are free: not covered, and open on the left or right side.
  2. Matched pairs leave the board and uncover the tiles below.
  3. Clear every tile to complete the level.

Whatever you call it, mahjong solitaire, mahjongg, Shanghai solitaire or tile matching, the idea is the same. Rules and first tips.
