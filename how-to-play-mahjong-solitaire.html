How to play mahjong solitaire

Mahjong solitaire is a one-player puzzle with mahjong tiles. The rules fit in three steps, and the only idea that takes a moment to learn is what makes a tile free.

Free to play. Plays offline, no account.

The three rules

  1. Tap two identical mahjong tiles that are free: not covered, and open on the left or right side.
  2. Matched pairs leave the board and uncover the tiles below.
  3. Clear every tile to complete the level.

That is the whole game. Mahjong solitaire is also called mahjongg, Shanghai solitaire, tile matching or pair matching, and it is a different game from the four-player mahjong that is played at a table.

What is a free tile?

A tile is free when two things are true. Nothing lies on top of it, and it is open on its left side or its right side. The end tiles of a row are usually free, and the tiles on top of a tall stack are free as long as nothing covers them. A tile that sits in the middle of a row, with a neighbour on both sides, is not free yet. A tile with another tile on top is not free either.

You can only match two tiles that are both free at the same moment. When a pair leaves the board, tiles that were blocked may become free, and that is how the board slowly opens up.

First tips for your first few boards

  • Look at the tall stacks first. Clearing tiles from the top of a stack frees the tiles beneath it, and a stack can hide the partners you need.
  • Prefer a match that uncovers new tiles over a match that changes nothing.
  • Work along a long row from its ends. Clearing an end tile can make the next tile in the row free.
  • Pinch to zoom if the tiles feel small. Mahjong365 uses large tiles with bold symbols and a unique picture on every pair.
  • Do not worry about a wrong move. Undo is unlimited and free in Mahjong365, and Shuffle is free as well.

Questions and answers

What is a free tile in mahjong solitaire?

A free tile is not covered by another tile and is open on its left or right side. You can match two identical tiles when both are free.

Is mahjong solitaire the same as Shanghai solitaire?

They are different names for the same kind of game: tap two identical free tiles to clear pairs until the board is empty. People also call it mahjongg or tile matching.
