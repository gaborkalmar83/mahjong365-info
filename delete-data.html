Delete your data. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

Mahjong365 has no account and no server of ours, so almost everything it stores is on your own device or in your own Google account. This page explains how to delete each part, and how to ask us to delete anything we might hold.

1. Data on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, streak, stickers, trophies and settings are stored only on your phone or tablet. To delete them: open Android Settings, Apps, Mahjong365, Storage, Clear storage (or simply uninstall the app).

2. Cloud save and leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your progress is also stored as a saved game in your Google account, and your leaderboard scores are shown under your Play Games profile name. This data is held by Google, not by us. To delete it: open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings, and choose Delete Play Games account and data (you can pick the Mahjong365 game only). More information: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/6325006.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads, and you can change your consent in the app under Settings, Privacy options. The one-time Premium purchase removes all ads.

4. Ask us to delete data

If you believe we hold any personal data about you, email kalmariumservices@gmail.com with the subject "Mahjong365 data deletion request". We will confirm within 30 days. In practice we hold no personal data ourselves, so we will tell you which of the steps above applies to you.
